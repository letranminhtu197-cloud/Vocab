Aurestar 16.16.42 — Itsuki Dialogue Persistence Fix

The floating Itsuki speech bubble no longer hides itself after a timer. It remains visible until you press ×. That hide choice is saved across reloads; click Itsuki to show the bubble again. “Quiet today” still snoozes the companion for the day.

Install: back up your data, extract this ZIP to a separate folder, open chrome://extensions, enable Developer mode, and select Load unpacked to choose the extracted folder. For an existing unpacked install, update the same extension folder to preserve its extension ID and access to its saved data.
