Aurestar 16.16.59 — Background Reminder Reliability

- Reconciles routine and idle-study alarms whenever the Manifest V3 service worker starts, in addition to install and browser startup.
- Preserves an existing one-minute recurring alarm instead of resetting its first tick on every reconciliation.
- Awaits alarm creation for the daily wake-up, wind-down, and sleep reminders.
- Recovers the focus-session reminder schedule after service-worker startup.
- Keeps the quote-only reminder page design from 16.16.58.

Important Chrome limitation:
- Manifest V3 background service workers are event-driven and may be suspended by Chrome; Chrome alarms wake the worker to handle scheduled events.
- Chrome must be running and the extension must remain enabled. Reminders cannot reliably open pages while Chrome is fully closed or the computer is asleep.
- Static checks do not substitute for testing the unpacked extension in Chrome.
